    Why Passphrases?

    Strong passwords are hard to remember, and memorable passwords are usually weak. Passphrases solve this problem by combining multiple random words into phrases that are both secure and memorable. This generator creates grammatically structured passphrases using a large vocabulary, resulting in high entropy (randomness) that makes them extremely difficult to crack.

    Each passphrase's strength is measured in bits of entropy, and every extra bit doubles the guesses an attacker needs. Around 50 bits holds up well where a site limits login attempts; for accounts that matter, use Extra Long. The surreal combinations (like "Crisp niece promises ruin") are intentionally unusual, making them both memorable and secure. You can customize separators, capitalization, and add numbers or symbols to meet any password requirements.

    Built using words curated from the EFF's passphrase wordlists, organized by part of speech for grammatical structure. Thank you to the EFF for their excellent work on password security.

    Privacy Notice: All passphrases are generated in your browser using cryptographically secure randomness. They never leave your device: nothing is sent to the server, logged, or stored. Each generation is completely random and independent.
